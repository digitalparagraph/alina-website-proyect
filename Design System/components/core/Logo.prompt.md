Lockups: \`primary\` (colour, SVG), \`byn\` (one colour, SVG), \`white\` (knockout PNG, dark fields).
Isotype badges (circular, for avatars and small placements): \`isoAmarillo\`, \`isoMorado\`, \`isoGris\`.
Minimum lockup height 56px so "existir consciente" stays legible. Clear space = the mark's circle diameter.
